Enable T-OTP

Step to enable T-OTP

  1. Download the T -OTP Authenticator App (Preferably google authenticator) and install the App on your mobile.
  2. Scan the QR code with the Authenticator App.
  3. App will validate and display the T-OTP.
  4. Enter the T-OTP and click on proceed.
Note: Please ensure that your mobile time matches with your zone.
